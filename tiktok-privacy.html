Privacy Policy

MINUSPLUS Social Publisher · Effective 11 August 2026

1. Information this workflow handles

When enabled and authorized by an account owner, this workflow may handle basic profile information made available by TikTok Login Kit, OAuth authorization data, and the media, captions, and metadata that the owner chooses to submit as drafts.

2. Why we use information

We use this information only to authenticate an authorized account, apply the permissions the owner granted, prepare or submit approved draft content, maintain operational security, and respond to support requests.

3. Storage and security

Access and refresh tokens are intended to be handled server-side only and are not embedded in public website source code or social post content. We use reasonable technical controls to limit access to authorized operators. No system can guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the social platform needed to perform an owner-authorized action, or when required to comply with law or protect rights and security.

5. Retention and deletion

Authorization data is retained only while needed for the connected workflow or until authorization is withdrawn. You may request deletion or revoke platform access through the relevant platform controls. To request assistance, contact us using the address below.

6. Your choices

You may decline requested permissions, revoke access in TikTok, and request information or deletion support by contacting us. Revoking access can prevent further draft submissions until a new authorization is granted.

7. Contact and updates

Questions or requests may be sent to erek.feng@gmail.com. We may update this policy when the workflow or applicable requirements change; the effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
